# RC2 Demo Final — presentation and operational handoff

TEST ONLY. Keep the approved sign-in, sidebar and institutional assets unchanged. No deployment to main, no production migration or official payments.

## Demonstration narrative

Curia creates a fresh OTC or QR rehearsal; each includes a fictional 50-form booklet, 10 previously issued and 2 previously voided, with a PHP 500 quote. Assign stock to the designated COWL demo account. COWL acknowledges it, records OTC or selects synthetic QR claim and matching credit (a mismatched credit is deliberately provided), issues the next unused receipt, and submits a cash-handover remittance. Curia independently checks the transfer and duplicate-copy inspection record and confirms or records a discrepancy. Reports show posted collections, remittance status, the receipt register and scoped transaction history. Opening stock accepts exact non-contiguous serials; prior use is not revenue. Repeat by creating another rehearsal, not deleting the audit ledger.

## Management value

Traceable custody and serial accountability; distinct COWL/Curia identities; verified collections separate from renewal approval; recorded remittance and independent acknowledgment; immutable evidence and scoped audit visibility. All records and amounts are fictional. No production staff, banking or payment account authorized.

## Release boundaries

QR evidence records are synthetic metadata only, not files or a real bank feed. No payment QR or actual funds. Watermarked specimen PDF is a draft for plain paper, not approved official stock, and calibration is browser-local. Physical print verification, real receiving accounts, evidence ingestion, private storage, institutional approvals, staff appointments, fee rules, production schema migration/recovery and signed user acceptance remain prerequisites for production. The 200-event audit view exposes only scoped TEST events and omits actor IDs and private metadata. Browser acceptance and WCAG review require an authenticated rehearsal. Preserve original demo records and permissions; no reset deletes history.
