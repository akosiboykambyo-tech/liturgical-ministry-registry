# RC3.3 — Flexible Collections and Supplemental Renewals
Approved 26 November 2026. Separate branch feature/rc33-flexible-renewals. TEST ONLY; keep production, encryption and RC3.2 branch untouched.

## Business contract
One parish cycle per year; independent original ministry applications; source online or walk-in; supplements linked to the original and separately assessed. Prior submitted/paid names, letters and payment status are recognized by assignment and year. Previously settled data stays read-only. Original compatible batches may be grouped; supplemental obligations are separate. Registry independently reviews each assignment and recommendation; COWL independently authorizes the complete obligation and verifies full settlement. Curia reconciliation remains independent of renewal validation.
